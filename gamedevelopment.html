Video Game Development

In our final topic for the year, I explored game design using GDevelop 5. I learned how to build playable games, working with event systems, logic, and mechanics to bring interactive ideas to life.

How to Play:

In this game, your goal is to navigate through all 3 levels and reach the door at the end before time runs out. Use WASD or the Arrow Keys to move left and right, and press Space, W, or the Up Arrow Key to jump. Make sure to watch your step. Avoid the spikes and try not to fall off the edge of the map, or you'll have to restart the level.
